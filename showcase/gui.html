§0 СВОДКА · One sheet. Two surfaces. One voice.

This page is the GUI half of the g2 design system: the tokens, the type, the frames, the stamps and the components every glory-to-* web surface draws from. Its terminal twin is Стандарт TUI. Both read the same seeds from tokens.css and tokens.toml, generated by one script that refuses to emit a pair of colours that fails WCAG AA.

ДОЗА · Dose

The owner's call on 2026-10-24: err toward excess and trim later. So frames go on containers, stamps go on status, dialogs are forms and the footer carries function keys. Principle 09 still holds: hover, focus, validation and reflow are not negotiable.

Treat the amount of bureau on this page as the ceiling, not the floor. A consuming app may dial down; it may not dial past this.

Palette lineage: glory-to-direktiva and the Obsidian Nirvytekh theme (warm near-black), glory-to-protocol (gold, stamp red, the four decisions), glory-to-gensek (token model, OKLab derivation, sage note).

Period grammar: CP866 box drawing, Norton Commander's F-key bar, GOST 6.39 form grids, ДСП classification corners. Modern homages: Papers, Please for restraint, Control for a bureau that is still a workplace. Sources in docs/references.md.

ЗАМЕТКА · why signal is split

Stamp red on the warm black measures about 3.4:1. Fine for a ring or a 20px heading, not for a sentence. Brightening it everywhere would stop it looking like ink, so body-size red gets its own lifted token, signal-text. Same reasoning gives faint a 3:1 target: it is for placeholders and disabled controls, never for words that must be read.

§2 ШРИФТ · Mono is the chrome. Prose is sans. Paper is serif.

JetBrains Mono carries every label, key, number, ledger and header, as it already does in the suite's SVGs. Body text is PT Sans, the Cyrillic-first public face, because paragraphs in monospace cost reading effort. On the paper theme the body switches to PT Serif, the typewritten-form register. All three are free and carry Cyrillic.

ГРАММАТИКА · label grammar

Cyrillic first, middle dot, English. СВОДКА · TL;DR, ОДОБРЕНО · APPROVED, ДСП · Экз. № 1. The Cyrillic is the state's voice and appears in every language; the English is for the citizen. In PT-BR surfaces the English slot takes Portuguese.

Uppercase is semantic. A focused panel title, a stamp, a classification mark. Never emphasis inside prose.

Real words only. Fake Cyrillic (Я for R) is out of canon, full stop.

МЕРА · measure & rhythm

Body measure 65ch. Line height 1.55 for sans, 1.6 for mono blocks. Scale ratio 1.25 on a 16px base; the mono logo tracks 0.18em, labels 0.12em.

Numbers in tables use tabular-nums. Dates are ISO (2026-10-24), document numbers zero-padded to three (№ 003).

§3 РАМКА · Weight carries state

The one grammar both surfaces share literally. In the terminal: ┌─┐ at rest, ┏━┓ focused, ╔═╗ for authority. Here: 1px, 2px gold, 4px double gold. A title sits in the top border, a kicker in the top-right. Never a shadow, never a radius.

┌─ rest 1px · border

A panel nobody is looking at. Secondary lists, previews, an inactive pane.

┏━ FOCUS 2px · accent

The pane with keyboard focus. Only one on screen at a time. Title goes uppercase.

╔═ AUTHORITY 4px double

The bureau speaking: a dialog, a directive, a result stamp's frame. Rare by design.

┈┈ pending dashed

Empty state, drop target, a form not yet filed.

§4 ПЕЧАТЬ · Four decisions and a seal

A stamp is a terminal decision on a request, never decoration. Rings, text on arcs, a glyph in the center, tilted a few degrees, with ink grain. The same four exist as text blocks in the TUI and as stamp_* helpers in glory-to-protocol. The chip is the stamp's small form for tables and lists.

ОДОБРЕНО · approved request granted, action complete
ОТКАЗАНО · rejected denied; always carries a reason
ПРИКАЗ · order the bureau dictates an action
К СВЕДЕНИЮ · review awaiting an external decision
ПРАВИЛА · rules

One stamp per record. A request has one terminal decision. Progress is shown with blocks, not stamps.

Color is the decision's. Approve takes ok, reject bad, order info, review warn. The seal alone takes signal or accent.

Tilt between −4° and −12°. A perfectly level stamp reads as a logo. Grain stays on; the ink filter is part of the component.

Never smaller than 96px as a ring. Below that, use the chip.

§5 ФОРМА · Brackets, boxes and one gold button

Inputs sit in a well with a bottom rule, flanked by mono brackets, the way Norton Commander drew [.........]. Checkboxes and radios are drawn as glyphs. One filled gold button per view; the rest are outline or ghost. Danger is red outline, never red fill.

КНОПКИ · buttons

Hover lifts the panel and gilds the border. Focus is a 2px bright-gold outline, offset 2px. Pressed state is the filled gold inverted (ink on gold).

ПРАВИЛА · rules

Labels above, mono, uppercase, tracked. Required marks a red asterisk. Help text sits below in mono xs.

Errors are literal. ✗, then what is wrong and what fixes it. No apology, no "oops".

The gold button is the filing act. Submit, approve, publish. Save-draft is outline. Destructive is red outline and asks once, as a form, not a browser confirm.

Buttons are sentence case. Uppercase is for stamps, chips, the focused frame and the classification corner; a button is an instruction, not a decree.

Keyboard hints on primary actions as a kbd inside the button. Mobile hides them.

§6 ВЕДОМОСТЬ · Fields in fixed positions, leaders between them

GOST 6.39 put every form field at a fixed coordinate. The ledger is that idea as a component: label, dotted leader, value, mono throughout. The table is the ledger with rows; the selected row carries a ▸, a rejected row a red wash, and status is always a chip.

§7 ДОКУМЕНТ · Case files, classification corners, redaction

Every public page is a numbered document. The header block names the document, its classification, who approved it and when. The ДСП corner sits top-right with a copy number, as the Soviet instruction placed it. Redacted spans are a bar the reader can still select, a nod to Control's case files: clearance is a hover away.

Body text goes back to sans here, because this is read, not scanned. Bold takes the bright ink, not red: red in prose is reserved for the state's words. Lists use the cursor glyph; task boxes are typed, as in the terminal.

Hierarchy from the period: ДСП < Секретно < Совершенно секретно. In practice the suite uses ДСП for internal, ОТКРЫТО for public, and Секретно as a joke it tells once.

ПРАВИЛА · rules

Document numbers are per repo, three digits, zero-padded. Standards use the СТАНДАРТ № series; everything else ДОКУМЕНТ №.

Dates are ISO. 2026-05-20 is the founding date on every first-generation document.

Redaction is content. Redact only what the joke needs; the real document underneath must still make sense without it.

§9 СИГНАЛ · Slips, blocks and a dialog that is a form

A toast is a filed slip with a coloured edge. Progress is a row of blocks. A confirmation is a small form inside an authority frame, with the question as a ledger and the answer as buttons. The empty state is a blank form, dashed, telling you what will appear and how to file the first entry.

ПЕЧАТЬ НА БУМАГЕ · print

Printing switches to the paper tokens by itself (@media print in the generated stylesheet), hides the index, the toolbar and any open slip, and keeps every frame at one pixel. The paper theme exists for this: a filed request should look filed when it comes out of the printer.

§11 ДОСТУП · Built in, then gated

The owner of this suite reads with dyslexia and ADHD. That is a design input, not a footnote: body text is sans with a generous line height, labels are short and placed where the eye lands, every state is also a shape, and nothing moves unless asked.

КОНТРАСТ

Body text ≥ 7:1, secondary text ≥ 4.5:1, placeholders and disabled ≥ 3:1. The generator exits non-zero otherwise; §1 recomputes it live.

ФОРМА, НЕ ЦВЕТ

Every state has a shape as well as a colour: stamps have glyphs, rows have ▸, errors start with ✗, frames have weight.

КЛАВИАТУРА

Focus is always visible (bright-gold outline). Primary actions show their key. Tab order follows the ledger: top to bottom, label to value.

ДВИЖЕНИЕ

No ambient animation. A stamp may land once on a decision; prefers-reduced-motion turns even that off.

ЧТЕНИЕ

Measure 65ch, paragraphs of two to four sentences, one idea each. Mono only where the eye scans, not where it reads.

ЯЗЫК

Cyrillic labels always pair with a word the reader knows. A Cyrillic-only element is decoration, and decoration is out of canon.

§12 ВНЕ КАНОНА · What the bureau does not do

The fastest way to make this look like a costume is any item in the left column. The right column is what the standard does instead.

✗Faux Cyrillic: ЯUSSIAИ, ИIRVYTEKHReal words, paired with English. Russian readers see "yaussiai" in the fake.
✗Hammer and sickle, red stars on everything, real states or leadersBorrowed grammar: F-keys, ДСП corner, form grid, numbered documents. One ★ in the masthead.
✗Rounded corners, drop shadows, gradients, glassmorphismFlat surfaces, a panel ramp, frame weight for depth.
✗Monospace paragraphsMono for chrome, PT Sans for prose, PT Serif on paper.
✗Gold and red on every elementField quiet. Gold is focus; red is a decision or a fault.
✗Stamps as decoration, stamps for progressOne stamp per record, only for a terminal decision. Blocks for progress.
✗CRT scanlines, screen curvature, flickerThe era is in type, frames and words. Not in making the screen worse.
✗Browser alerts and confirmsA dialog is a form inside an authority frame.
✗Light mode by OS preferencePaper is a theme the user picks, not a mode the system flips.
✗Anything from Arstotzka or the FBCAtmosphere only. Names, symbols and language are the suite's own.
