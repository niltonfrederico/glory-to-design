§0 СВОДКА · One glance, one action, one stamp

This is the third surface of the g2 design system, after the GUI and the terminal. Same seeds, same token names, same stamps, same label grammar. What changes is the canvas: a 480 × 480 disc on the wrist, read for about five seconds at a time, often in ambient light, often with one hand busy. Every disc on this page is drawn only with the tokens in §1, so the paper toggle above re-renders the whole wrist.

ТРИ ПРАВИЛА · the three rules

1. One screen, one decision. A disc shows one document and asks one question. The answer is a stamp. Anything that needs a second question is a second screen, reached by the bezel.

2. The bezel is the cursor. Rotating steps the ▸ through a list or a stack of cards, exactly as j/k do in the terminal. Tap selects. Swipe right is back. The screen never asks for a scrollbar.

3. The field stays dark. On an OLED a lit pixel is the expense. The warm near-black costs almost nothing, so the bureau's palette is also the battery policy. Always-on keeps under fifteen percent of the pixels lit.

ПРИМЕЧАНИЕ · note

The wrist is the only surface where the bureau's square frames meet a round canvas. The decision: frames stay square and sit inside the circle. The circle is the hardware, like the bezel of a monitor; the standard does not bend its forms to it. Rim text, curved along the edge, is allowed on the watch face only (§3).

§1 МАСШТАБ · Same tokens, wrist scale

No new colour exists for the watch. The token table is the one in tokens/tokens.css and tokens.toml; §2 adds a usage rule for always-on, not a token. What is new is the scale. The Galaxy Watch6 Classic 47 mm is 480 × 480 physical pixels at density 2, so the design grid is 240 × 240 dp and every disc on this page is drawn at 1 CSS px = 1 dp.

§2 ДЕЖУРНЫЙ ЭКРАН · Always-on is a usage rule, not a theme

When the wrist drops, the screen goes to its duty state. The budget is fifteen percent of the pixels, counted by brightness, so the bureau's duty screen is text on well: the time in regular-weight gold, one ledger line, nothing filled, no stamp. Paper has no duty state; paper is for reading, not for waiting.

ПРАВИЛО · the duty rule

Allowed on duty: accent, muted, faint, one glyph. Not allowed: accent fills, selection wash, stamps, the waveform, any colour of state.

Measure with the AOD analyzer in Watch Face Studio before publishing a face. A disc that passes here can still fail there if the white-pixel reading is used; keep headroom.

§3 ЦИФЕРБЛАТ · The watch face is a document header

A bureau form opens with a framed ledger: number, title, directorate, date. The watch face is that header with the time where the title goes. Two complications at most, as ledger rows, fed by the apps below. The rim carries the date in the state's voice; the inscribed square carries everything the citizen reads.

§4 ПЛИТКИ · A tile is one sheet of the ledger

Tiles sit one bezel turn to the right of the face. Each is a single filed sheet: a Cyrillic title with its English twin, one figure or one list of three, one 48 dp action. A tile never scrolls; if it needs to, it is an app.

§5 ЗАМЕТКА · Voice notes are dictated and filed

The citizen dictates; the bureau files. Three screens: recording, the filed slip, the shelf of notes. The waveform is the TUI's block ramp standing up. Transcription, when it exists, arrives later on the phone; the watch only promises a timestamp and a file number.

§6 КАЗНА · A payment reminder is a direct order

Money on the wrist is a reminder, never a ledger to browse. The notification is a ПРИКАЗ: the state tells the citizen that a sum is due, with a date, and offers exactly two answers. Paid files it with an ОДОБРЕНО. Defer refiles it for tomorrow and says so. Amounts are the one place the figure size is mandatory.

§7 УВЕДОМЛЕНИЯ · Every notification is a filed slip

One shape for all of them: a Cyrillic label with its state colour, a 14 sp line in PT Sans, one meta row, up to two 48 dp actions. The label grammar is the stamp grammar. Haptics are part of the shape and are written down, because colour never carries state alone on the wrist either. The top label is capped at 18 characters (§1), and the English twin is the first thing to go, so a slip may read ✗ ОТКАЗАНО alone; the body sentence carries the rest.

§8 ДЕЛА · Tasks are checked, never typed

The watch adds no task. It checks, defers and dictates. The list is the tile's list, longer, stepped by the bezel; the detail is a single sheet with the two answers. A new task is a voice note that the phone promotes.

§9 НАДЗОР · System watch is a status board, read at arm's length

Servers, jobs, the home cluster: one row each, glyph plus word plus colour, the terminal's status line folded five times. No graphs. A sparkline at 240 dp is a rumour; a number with a trend glyph is a fact.

§10 ВВОД · The input ledger

Every gesture on the Classic maps to one terminal gesture. The mapping is fixed across apps, so a citizen who learned the TUI already knows the watch.

§11 ВНЕ КАНОНА · What the wrist does not do

The GUI and TUI lists apply. These are the watch-specific ways to turn the standard into a costume, or the battery into a casualty.

✗Rounded cards because the screen is roundSquare frames inside the circle. The case is round; the standard is not.
✗Curved text anywhere but the face's rim dateStraight lines in the inscribed square. Curved labels are decoration.
✗A filled gold or red field on always-onText on well, regular weight, under fifteen percent lit.
✗Three actions on one discTwo at most, primary left, always the same pair for the same question.
✗Charts, sparklines, rings of progressA figure, a trend glyph, ten blocks. The ring is the bezel's job.
✗Typing on the wristDictate, check, defer. Text is written on the phone.
✗Red for an overdue amountRed is a rejection. Overdue is К СВЕДЕНИЮ in warn.
✗Celebration screens, confetti, streaksA stamp and one sentence. The Directorate notes compliance.
✗Hex codes in ComposeG2Bureau.accent, from the generated tokens.kt.
✗A paper theme on always-onDuty state is always the bureau. Paper is for reading with the wrist raised.
